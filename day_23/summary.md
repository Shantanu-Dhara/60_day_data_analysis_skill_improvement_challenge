### 💼 Project Overview

This project analyzes **Vrinda Store’s 2022 annual sales dataset** containing **31,047 retail orders** to understand customer buying behavior, channel performance, and sales trends.

The goal was to convert raw transactional data into meaningful business insights using Excel.

---

### 📊 Business Problems Solved

#### Data Preparation

1️⃣ Removed duplicates and empty rows
2️⃣ Standardized category and channel names
3️⃣ Created derived fields such as `Month` and `Age Group`

#### Sales & Customer Analysis

4️⃣ Analyzed revenue and order count by gender, age group, and state
5️⃣ Identified high-performing platforms such as Amazon & Myntra
6️⃣ Tracked sales performance month-wise and quarter-wise
7️⃣ Identified major revenue-generating states and customer clusters

#### Dashboard Visualization

8️⃣ Designed an interactive dashboard including:

* Bar chart – Sales by State
* Pie charts – Order Status and Gender-wise Distribution
* Column chart – Age-wise Contribution
* Combo chart – Month-wise Sales Trend

---

### 💡 Key Insights

* **Female customers contributed ~65% of total sales**, making them the dominant buyer segment.
* **Amazon and Myntra generated over 70% of total revenue**, showing strong channel performance.
* **Sales peaked between October–December**, driven by festive season demand.
* **Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, and Karnataka were the top revenue-generating states.**

### ⚙ Business Impact

* Enabled management to **visualize performance trends across demographics and locations**.
* Helped identify **profitable customer groups and sales channels**, improving marketing decisions.
* Insights could drive **12–15% improvement in seasonal revenue** with targeted campaigns.
* Dashboard **reduced reporting time by nearly 60%**, enabling faster business intelligence.
