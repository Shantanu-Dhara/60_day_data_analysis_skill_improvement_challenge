### 📝 Key Problems Solved  

1. Find total spending of each customer (JOIN + Aggregation)
2. Find the most expensive order per customer (Window Function – ROW_NUMBER)
3. Find customers who spent above the average spending 
4. Find each product's rank based on total quantity sold (Window Function – RANK)
5. Find customers who have never ordered anything (LEFT JOIN + NULL check)
