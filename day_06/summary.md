### 🧠 Topics Practiced
**SQL Joins Covered:**

#### 🔹 INNER JOIN
- Show each student’s name along with their department name.  
- Display the student name and marks.  
- Show department name and the names of students in that department.

#### 🔹 LEFT JOIN
- Display all students and their department names (including those without a department).  
- List all students and their marks, even if marks are missing.

#### 🔹 RIGHT JOIN
- Show all departments and their student names (including empty ones).  
- Display all students’ marks, even if not matched to any student.

#### 🔹 FULL OUTER JOIN
- Show all students and departments, including unmatched records.  
- Combine student and result data, showing all whether matched or not.

#### 🔹 CROSS JOIN
- Display all possible combinations of students and departments.  
- Generate a list of all possible student-department pairs.

#### 🔹 SELF JOIN
- Display each employee with their manager’s name.  
- Show all employees who are managers.  
- Find the employee-manager hierarchy.
