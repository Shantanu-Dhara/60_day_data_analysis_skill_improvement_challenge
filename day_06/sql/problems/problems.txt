🧩 PRACTICE QUESTIONS BY JOIN TYPE

🟢 INNER JOIN

Q1) Show each student’s name along with their department name.

Q2) Display the student name and marks (use Students and Results tables).

Q3) Show department name and the names of students who belong to that department.

🟣 LEFT JOIN

Q1) Display all students and their department names — include those who don’t belong to any department.

Q2) List all students and their marks, even if some students don’t have marks yet.

🟠 RIGHT JOIN

Q1) Show all departments and their student names (include departments with no students).

Q2) Display all students’ marks from the Results table — even if some marks don’t belong to a valid student.

🔵 FULL OUTER JOIN

Q1) Show all students and departments — include even those without any match.

Q2) Combine student and result data to show all students and all marks, whether matched or not.

💡 (If you use MySQL, use LEFT JOIN + RIGHT JOIN + UNION to simulate FULL OUTER JOIN)

🟡 CROSS JOIN

Q1) Display all possible combinations of students and departments.

Q2) Generate a list of all possible student-department pairs (useful to simulate assigning students to every department).

🪞 SELF JOIN

Q1) Using the Managers table, display each employee with their manager’s name.

Q2) Show all employees who are managers of someone.

Q3) Find the employee-manager hierarchy (who reports to whom).
